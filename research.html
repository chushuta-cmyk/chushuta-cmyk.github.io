PROJECT — 01 COMPUTATIONAL NEUROSCIENCE & EEG

Parameterized Resting-State EEG Signatures of Acute Sleep Deprivation

Investigating cortical state reactivity and individual neural trait stability under acute sleep deprivation across 71 paired participants (142 sessions, OpenNeuro ds004902). By excising the 1/f aperiodic background via spectral parameterization (FOOOF/specparam), we demonstrated marked aperiodic broadband offset elevation (dz = +0.788) alongside robust Individual Alpha Frequency (IAF) stability (ICC = 0.816). Implemented subject-independent Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO) machine learning decoding (SVM AUC = 0.881) and out-of-fold vigilance prediction (r = 0.750, R2 = 0.551).

Resting-State EEG FOOOF / specparam Aperiodic 1/f Dynamics MNE-Python LOSO Machine Learning Functional Connectivity
Status: Manuscript Finalized (Draft v15, submission ready to SLEEP) · Lead Author
PROJECT — 02 COMPUTATIONAL PSYCHIATRY & MULTI-OMICS

MDD–OXPHOS Multi-Omics Re-analysis Framework

A comprehensive cross-tissue, multi-omics retrospective re-evaluation integrating 31 automated R/Python pipelines across 13 clinical and postmortem cohorts (analytic N > 2,000). Reassessing asserted mitochondrial and oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) dysregulation in Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) across peripheral blood transcriptomics (6 cohorts), postmortem brain transcriptomics (4 brain regions: DLPFC, AnCg, nAcc, HIP), paired Illumina EPIC 850K DNA methylation, and statistical genetics (GWAS summary statistics, FUMA/MAGMA, SMR). Revealed that previous reports of widespread OXPHOS downregulation conflate somatic comorbidity, medication exposure, and cell composition shifts.

RNA-seq Microarray DNA Methylation (EPIC 850K) GWAS & MAGMA Cell Deconvolution Psychiatric Genomics
Status: Complete Pipeline Release (31 Reproducible Modules) · Working Paper Ready
PROJECT — 03 VIRAL ONCOLOGY & IMMUNOGENOMICS

EBV Genomic Stratification & Myeloid-Inflammatory Polarization in NPC

Multi-omics investigation establishing how pre-treatment plasma Epstein–Barr virus (EBV) DNA copy number stratifies distinct tumor immune landscapes in nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC). Integrating bulk transcriptomic profiling with stage-adjusted sensitivity analysis, immune deconvolution (CIBERSORT, MCP-counter), and single-cell RNA-seq (NPC43). Demonstrated that low-EBV DNA tumors mount an intense, stage-robust myeloid-inflammatory signature (CXCL8, S100A8/A9, TREM1) driven by tumor-intrinsic transcriptional programs, whereas high-EBV tumors exhibit profound adaptive immune attenuation.

EBV DNA Stratification Single-Cell RNA-seq DESeq2 CIBERSORT Tumor Microenvironment Survival Modeling
Status: Completed Follow-up Study (Draft 12, Co-first Author) & Published Review (Viruses 2025)
PROJECT — 04 NEUROIMAGING & BIOMARKER META-ANALYSIS

Multimodal Biomarker & Coordinate-Based fMRI Meta-Analytic Programs

Dual meta-analytic investigations targeting critical questions in neuropsychiatry: (1) Amyloid-β in Depression: A 24-study systematic review and meta-analysis synthesizing CSF, PET, and plasma biomarkers, revealing a near-null pooled difference overall but uncovering crucial quantification discordance between DVR kinetic modeling and SUVR (published in Neuroscience, 2026); and (2) Childhood Adversity & Reward Processing: First coordinate-based Activation Likelihood Estimation (ALE) meta-analysis evaluating task-based fMRI across reward circuits (striatum, mPFC), demonstrating literature-wide statistical power deficits and spatial convergence boundaries.

Activation Likelihood Estimation (ALE) Task fMRI PET / CSF / Plasma Biomarkers PRISMA Guidelines Statistical Power Analysis
Status: Published in Neuroscience (2026) · Under Review at Child Abuse & Neglect · First Author
